The **surya** kernel (Poco X3 & X3 NFC, unified) just got its monthly maintenance drop.

## Highlights

- Rebased onto the latest **Linux-stable**
- Merged newest **CAF** tags for the SM7150 platform
- Assorted **battery** and **thermal** tuning
- Fixes for a couple of long-standing wake-up regressions

Grab the build from our [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/projects/stormbreakerdevices/files/)
or build it yourself from
[`kernel_xiaomi_surya`](https://github.com/stormbreaker-project/kernel_xiaomi_surya).

As always — flash responsibly, and report issues on our tracker. 🙏
