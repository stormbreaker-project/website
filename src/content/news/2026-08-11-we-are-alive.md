Short version, for everyone who kept asking: **StormBreaker is not dead.**

We went quiet for a stretch. Life pulled contributors in different directions, releases slowed, and the group chats got calm. But the sources never moved, the kernels kept booting, and a lot of you stuck around anyway. We just needed to catch our breath.

Now we are catching up.

## A new home

This site is the first step. The kernels, the device list, and this newsroom now live under one roof at [stormbreaker.squadri.me](https://stormbreaker.squadri.me), with a dark and light theme that remembers your choice. Every post here is just a Markdown file in the [website repo](https://github.com/stormbreaker-project/website), so shipping an update is a commit, not a CMS.

## StormBreaker Clang is back

Our in-house LLVM/Clang toolchain is in active development again, compiling our kernels the way we like them: `make CC=clang LLVM=1`, no shortcuts.

## The work is moving

LTS bumps, CLO merges, and real backports are landing across the maintained trees. We upstream the parts OEMs freeze at launch, so your phone keeps getting better long after the box gets thrown away.

## Come build with us

Maintainers, testers, and the curious are all welcome. Report a bug, request a device, or ship your first commit.

> The storm never really stopped. It was just recharging. ⚡
