Our story

About Team StormBreaker

A volunteer community building custom Android kernels, in the open, since October 2019.

Team StormBreaker began in October 2019 as a small group of enthusiasts building better kernels for the phones we actually carried. What started with a handful of devices grew into a name known across XDA and the Telegram custom kernel scene, with over a million downloads, a team of device maintainers, and deep upstream work. Life eventually pulled contributors in different directions and things slowed down, but the sources, the community and the legacy are all still here.

October 2019

The first spark

Team StormBreaker launches with our first batch of devices. A small group of enthusiasts building better kernels for the phones we actually carried.

Late 2019 to 2020

It caught fire

Our releases spread fast across Telegram communities and XDA, and StormBreaker quickly became a familiar name in the custom kernel scene, on its way to over a million downloads.

2020 to 2021

A growing team

A handful of maintainers joined to bring StormBreaker to more and more devices, turning a side project into a real community effort.

2021 to 2023

Deep under the hood

LTS merges, ACK merges, CLO merges, backports, upstream releases and Custom ROM integrations. The serious kernel work we became known for.

Along the way

StormBreaker Clang

We built our own optimized Clang toolchain to compile our kernels. After a quiet spell it is back in active development.

2024 to today

The lull, and what comes next

As life pulled contributors in different directions, active development and community support slowed. Everything we built is still here, and we are rebuilding, starting with this new home.
