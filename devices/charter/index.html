How we maintain devices

We maintain kernel lines, not one kernel per phone. A shared patchset is kept in one place and propagates across every device on a line, and the whole line ships together as a release train.

01

Two axes

Every device inherits from an upstream LTS line (stable merges, CVEs, generic patches) and a SoC-common tree (vendor drivers). We maintain both, not one kernel per phone.

02

One shared patchset

It lives in a single source and propagates to every device on a line: backports, scheduler and network tuning, hardening, and add-on modules like srandom. Vendor patches stay inside their chipset group.

03

Release trains

Devices on a line ship together at a tagged patchset level, sb-<version>-rN, so "every 4.19 device is at r7" is a precise, bisectable statement.

04

CI gate

A common tag is promoted only after the lead device boots and the whole line builds green, so one bad merge never reaches a release.
